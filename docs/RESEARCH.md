## Reference studies

- **Ocean School** — https://oceanschool.nfb.ca: the fetch timed out; followed the supplied dark immersive hero and restrained motion description. Our hero uses a local image, typographic reveal and transform-only parallax instead of a heavy initial video payload.
- **Manhattan School of Music** — https://www.msmnyc.edu: clear grouped navigation and an admissions route in the menu. Our design uses a large editorial mega-menu with a secondary visual and a persistent tour link.
- **The Walker School** — https://www.thewalkerschool.org: a school story has to make student life concrete. The supplied horizontal-story concept becomes three original chapters with desktop pinning and stacked mobile reading.
- **VCUarts** — https://arts.vcu.edu: a consistent, unexpected graphic idea can provide identity. Here jade is the recurring material; a lighter jade layer gradually crossfades as the page progresses. Only opacity changes, not layout or animated background-colour paints.
- **Salesian College** — https://www.salesian.vic.edu.au: primary actions include Enrol and Book a Tour. Our header retains the tour action at every breakpoint, including when the menu is open.
- **International Grammar School Sydney** — https://www.igssyd.nsw.edu.au: distinct school identity, a warm welcome and published stories. Our outcomes block uses an explicit, manual two-story slider with source links and no autoplay.

## Decisions

1. **A school, separate from the preschool.** A jade/off-white editorial identity and original faceted mark replace the preschool's playful garden direction.
2. **A parent journey.** The page moves from educational approach to programmes, student life, evidence/stories, and direct admissions contact. No scrolling instructions or decorative filler slogans are used as navigation.
3. **Typography leads.** Self-hosted Newsreader and DM Sans, strong contrast in scale, quiet labels, thin rules, few borders and no repeated rounded-card grid.
4. **Honest evidence.** Empty statistics use em dashes and explicit TODOs. Counter infrastructure animates only supplied, verified numbers. Alumni destinations and fees remain TODO.
5. **Photography is provisional.** Only accessible, school-specific archive photographs are used. Repeated crops are temporary and visibly labelled; the replacement schedule explains the needed shoot.
6. **No dead Phase 2 links.** The Phase 1 menu points to implemented Home sections. New pages will replace those targets after review.
7. **A real validation path, a stub transport.** Shared validation runs in the browser and route handler. A valid result explicitly says no email has been sent or saved.
