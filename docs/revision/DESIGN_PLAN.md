# Expanded school design

## Brand system

The complete original logo stays on white. Crest purple #431448 is the main colour for the masthead, programme section, major admissions panels and footer. Leaf green #29B14D marks selected controls and tour actions, with black text. Shield grey #E8E8EA, white #FFFFFF, black #000000 and a purple/white tint #F6F3F6 complete the six-colour system. Sampling and provenance remain in content/brand.md.

DM Sans 600 gives headings a clear, consistent voice. DM Sans 400 handles descriptions, facts and navigation. Both are self-hosted. Headings use one colour and weight, without accent words or tracked all-caps labels.

## Three principles

1. Make the brand visible. Use genuine purple across complete surfaces, with the unmodified crest in its white panel.
2. Show Jade's specific spaces and activities. Circular tables, glass writing walls, practical science, the computer room and school court carry the design.
3. Make interaction useful. Programme tabs, campus photograph selection, gallery filters and a tour message planner help a family explore and take a next step.

## Expansion and critique

The limited Step 3 site was too sparse and its purple was barely visible. The expanded website adds substantial About, Learning, School life, Admissions, News and Contact pages alongside the individual study routes. Home includes classroom details, campus exploration, real activities, student testimony, school stories and visit guidance.

A generic row of feature cards would have repeated the previous problem. Instead, the classroom section uses two differently scaled photographs, programmes use a large image/text panel, school life uses an overlapping photographic composition, campus exploration uses selectable rooms, stories use an editorial grid, and admissions uses actual sequential steps. No fabricated counters, awards or decorative numbering fill space.

One decorative circle behind the contact panel was removed during review. Photograph focal points were adjusted so the About and preschool introductory crops preserve faces. The gallery dialog was explicitly centred and its selected image loads eagerly. Mobile programme tabs were refined into one three-column row.

The archive photographs vary in quality. Keep their natural colour and real setting. A future approved school shoot can replace the softer originals without changing this layout.
