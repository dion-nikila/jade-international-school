Before you visit.

More admissions questions
Which stage can my child join?+

Jade publishes preschool, primary, IGCSE, A Level and Fast Track routes, alongside the NCC International Foundation Diploma. Contact admissions with your child’s age and current stage to discuss an appropriate entry point.

Can we see the school before applying?+

Yes. Contact the school to arrange a tour and confirm the section you would like to visit. A requested time is confirmed directly by the school.

How do we confirm subjects and fees?+

Ask admissions for the current subject list, examination board, timetable and written fee schedule for your chosen programme. Availability and entry requirements should be confirmed for your intended intake.
