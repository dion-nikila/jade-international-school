Get to know Jade.

An international school in Colombo with workshop-style lessons, circular tables and spaces for students to work together.

Our classroomsTeachingSchool life

A classroom built for conversation.

At Jade, students work around circular tables and use the glass walls as boards. The room is part of the lesson.

Work at the same table

Circular tables bring students together to work through problems and compare ideas.

Put the thinking on the wall

Glass boards make room for students to explain a method, sketch a plan and discuss the next step.

Learn through participation

Workshop-style teaching makes discussion and active involvement part of the lesson.

About teaching at Jade

What learning looks like here.

Participation is central to Jade’s classroom approach. Students share their work, explain ideas and learn with one another.

Discussion

Students and teachers share the table. Questions and explanations become part of the classroom conversation.

Practical work

Science experiments, computer work and group tasks give students different ways to work with the subject.

Shared thinking

Glass boards and large working surfaces give ideas a visible place in the room.

Meet the teaching approach.

Siraj Hanes teaches Economics and Business at IGCSE and International A Level. His teaching interests include real-world examples and the way classroom environments affect learning.

For families, a visit is an opportunity to discuss how a subject is taught and how the programme fits the student’s current stage.
