Learning / From the school

Workshop-style learning brings students into the discussion. At Jade, glass-walled classrooms and circular tables give them space to share work and explore the ideas in a lesson.

Students write on glass boards, plan on large sheets of paper and work around shared tables. Visit Jade to see the rooms and discuss how lessons are organised.
